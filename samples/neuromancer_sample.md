# Chapter 1: The Sky Above the Port

The sky above the port was the color of television, tuned to a dead channel.

"It's not like I'm using," Case said, leaning against the damp neon-lit counter of the Chatsubo. "My nervous system is burned out. I can't jack in."

Linda Lee looked at him through the smoky violet haze of the bar. "You're chasing ghosts, Case. Night City will chew you up and spit out whatever chrome you have left."

"I have a contact in the Sprawl," he muttered, watching the bartender pour another draft. "An Ono-Sendai cyberdeck with military-grade icebreakers."

"Be careful," she whispered, touching the synthetic skin of his jacket. "The corporations own the matrix. They own everything."

Case turned toward the rain-streaked window. Below in the alley, holographic advertisements flickered against the rusted iron fire escapes, painting the wet pavement in strokes of electric cyan and phosphor green.

# Chapter 2: The Coffin Racks

The cheap hotel was a honeycomb of fiberglass capsules stacked six high against the blind brick wall of an old warehouse.

"Ten credits for the night," the clerk rasped, not looking up from his pocket terminal.

Case slotted the plastic card into the console. The magnetic lock clicked open with a sharp hydraulic hiss. Inside the capsule, the cooling fan hummed a low, steady drone—the white noise of twenty-first century solitude.
